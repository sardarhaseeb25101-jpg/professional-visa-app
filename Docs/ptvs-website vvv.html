Home / Wakala

Wakala (E-Wakala) Services

Wakala, also called E-Wakala or visa authorization, is the online authorization a Saudi employer issues to a licensed Pakistani agency so that agency can process the visa. Without a Wakala in an agency's name, the visa cannot be stamped through that agency.

What we do

  • Give your Saudi employer our agency details exactly as they must appear on the Wakala
  • Check the Wakala once issued: visa number, profession, number of visas and agency name
  • Use the Wakala to complete Wafid medical, Tasheer biometrics, visa stamping and protector for each worker
  • Handle single visas and bulk Wakala for companies hiring several workers

What we need

  • Visa number(s) and the Saudi employer's name
  • Copy of the issued Wakala
  • Each worker's passport and CNIC copies

Ask your employer to issue the Wakala in our name, then send it to us on WhatsApp and we take it from there.

Home / Attestation

Saudi Embassy Attestation

Every education document is first attested by the body that issued or regulates it, then by MOFA Pakistan (Lahore / Peshawar / Islamabad), before any embassy attestation. No visa or job letter is needed for most documents; you can attest in advance.

For employers: how it works

  1. Send your requirement: job titles, number of workers, salary, contract period and benefits.
  2. Demand letter and power of attorney: issued in our agency's name, attested as required.
  3. E-Wakala: you issue the visa authorization in our agency's name.
  4. Sourcing and shortlisting: we advertise, screen and shortlist matching candidates.
  5. Interviews and trade tests: interview online or in person; we arrange practical trade tests.
  6. Skill verification: skilled workers pass the Takamol SVP test; professionals complete degree attestation and QVP.
  7. Medical: workers complete the Wafid (GAMCA) medical at approved centres.
  8. Visa stamping and protector: Tasheer biometrics, visa stamping, and each worker's protector and OEC.
  9. Deployment: we book flights and send each worker's travel details before arrival.

Documents from the employer

  • Demand letter on company letterhead (trades, numbers, salary, contract period)
  • Power of attorney in favour of our agency
  • Employment contract / agreement for each trade
  • Copy of company Commercial Registration (CR)
  • Visa block / visa numbers
  • E-Wakala in our agency's name

Documents from each worker

  • Valid passport and CNIC
  • Recent passport-size photos with white background
  • Experience or trade certificate, if available
  • Takamol skill verification certificate (we arrange the test)
  • Wafid medical fitness report (we arrange the appointment)

Family Umrah Visa for Saudi Arabia

Perform Umrah together as a family

We arrange the Umrah visa for every family member, register you on Nusuk, book hotels and transport, and check each person's documents and vaccinations before you fly.

  • Passport valid for at least 6 months from entry (we recommend 8 months)
  • Two recent passport-size photos, plain white background
  • CNIC copy (adults) or B-Form / birth certificate (children)
  • Nikah Nama or Marriage Registration Certificate for husband and wife
  • Meningitis (ACWY) vaccination, at least 10 days before arrival
  • Polio vaccination certificate (mandatory from Pakistan) on the NADRA card
  • Confirmed return ticket and hotel booking (arranged with your package)
Family rules
Women of any age can perform Umrah without a mahram. Children travel with a parent or legal guardian. Up to 7 family members per Nusuk account.
Validity
Follows your package dates, maximum stay 90 days. Medical insurance included.
Processing
Usually 1 to 3 working days after all documents are ready; longer in Ramadan and peak season.
